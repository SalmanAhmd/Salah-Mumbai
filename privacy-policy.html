1. About Salah Mumbai

Salah Mumbai ("the App") is a prayer and Salah companion application designed to help users manage daily prayers, prayer reminders, prayer history, and goals.

We respect your privacy. Salah Mumbai does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

Salah Mumbai does not collect personal information from you.

The App does not require:

  • Login or registration
  • Name, email address, or phone number
  • A user account
  • Cloud storage or data synchronization
  • Location information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Analytics or tracking data

3. Location Information

Salah Mumbai does not request or access your device's location.

Location permissions are explicitly excluded from the Android application configuration as an additional safeguard against a future dependency unintentionally requesting location access.

We therefore do not collect, store, transmit, or share your location.

4. Prayer History, Goals, and Reminders

The App may store information such as prayer history, Salah goals, prayer reminders, and related app preferences.

This information is stored locally on your Android device using a local SQLite database.

This information:

  • Is not uploaded to any server
  • Is not stored in the cloud
  • Is not synchronized between devices
  • Is not accessible to us
  • Is not shared with third parties

If you uninstall the App or clear its application data, locally stored information may be deleted by Android.

5. Notifications and Android Permissions

Salah Mumbai uses local Android notifications to remind you about prayers and other activities. Notifications are scheduled and managed locally on your device.

The App does not use push notification services and does not collect push notification tokens.

POST_NOTIFICATIONS
Used to display prayer reminders and other notifications.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM
Used to schedule reminders at their intended times.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED
Used to restore scheduled reminders after the device restarts.
VIBRATE
Used to allow notifications to provide vibration feedback.

These permissions are used solely for the App's notification and reminder functionality.

6. Analytics and Tracking

Salah Mumbai does not use:

  • Analytics services
  • Advertising services
  • Crash-reporting services
  • Tracking technologies
  • Advertising identifiers
  • User profiling

We do not track how you use the App.

7. Advertising

Salah Mumbai does not display advertisements and does not use advertising SDKs.

We do not sell or share user information with advertisers.

8. Third-Party Services

Salah Mumbai does not transmit your personal information to third-party services.

The App does not use third-party services for analytics, advertising, user tracking, crash reporting, cloud synchronization, or user accounts.

The Android operating system may provide standard system-level functionality and services outside the control of Salah Mumbai. Such system services are governed by Google's applicable policies.

9. Data Security

Because Salah Mumbai does not transmit your personal information to our servers, we do not maintain a Salah Mumbai server-side database containing your personal information.

Prayer history, goals, reminders, and related app data remain on your device.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your Android device.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Salah Mumbai does not maintain a server-side copy of your data.

Data stored locally by the App remains on your device until you delete it, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App, subject to Android's handling of application data.

Because we do not collect or maintain personal information on our servers, there is no server-side account or personal-data deletion request required.

11. Children's Privacy

Salah Mumbai does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Because the App does not require an account and does not collect personal information, it can be used without providing personal information to us.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's functionality or privacy practices change.

Any updated Privacy Policy will be published at this URL, and the Effective Date at the top of this page will be updated accordingly.

We encourage users to periodically review this Privacy Policy.
